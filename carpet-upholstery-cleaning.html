Advanced carpet, upholstery & hard floor cleaning

Our Texatherm® trained technicians can clean carpets, upholstery and hard floors using multiple cleaning methods, all from just one machine. They can adapt immediately whilst on site, switching between cleaning methods and processes in minutes, depending on our client’s needs.

These methods include

  • Hot water extraction
  • Texatherm® low moisture cleaning
  • Texatherm® dual combination cleaning
  • Restorative cleaning
  • Hard floor cleaning
  • Upholstery and sofa cleaning

Hot water extraction

The hot water extraction (HWE) cleaning method is suitable for cleaning most carpets, sofas, upholstery and other textiles, as long as they are not water sensitive.

To get the best performance from a carpet extraction machine, it needs to be able to pull air flow through the carpet backing. However, not all carpet types allow for this.

Office carpet tiles, for example, are constructed with watertight backings, which stop any airflow from being pulled through the backing and up the carpet fibres. This lack of airflow dramatically reduces the performance of a hot water extraction machine, so a low moisture system may be better suited and will give superior results.

Low moisture cleaning

The Texatherm® low moisture cleaning process is a great alternative to HWE. It uses a combination of chemicals that react with an absorbent heated pad to create a capillary wicking action that draws dirt and stains out of the carpet fibres and onto the pad.

This method does not need airflow through the carpet backing to work effectively, so it gives safer, superior cleaning results on commercial carpet tiles that dry very quickly. The process is also much quieter and dramatically reduces cleaning time when compared with HWE cleaning.

Benefits of low moisture cleaning

  • A superior clean on synthetic commercial carpet tiles
  • Cleaning time reduced by up to 60% when compared to conventional cleaning methods
  • A more environmentally friendly way to clean carpets: water consumption is cut by up to 80%, and 80% less foul water returns to drains
  • Low noise means less disruption in the workplace
  • A low moisture clean, with all the benefits normally associated with a dry cleaning system
  • 30 minute drying time
