Deep Cleaning across Merseyside & the North West

We know your time is precious, so let us take care of your domestic deep cleaning needs by getting into all the nooks and crannies around your home.

Our deep clean is

  • A tailored service to all our cleaning customers
  • A top to bottom thorough ‘Spring’ clean, guaranteed to get your home in tip-top shape
  • Ideal before selling your property or if you’ve just moved into your new home
  • Perfect to get your home ready before your new baby arrives

No need to move belongings

Deep clean services are done with all your belongings in place, whilst reaching those neglected areas during the cleaning process.

What’s included

  • All rooms

    We vacuum and mop floors, clean doors and frames, polish door handles, remove cobwebs, wipe skirting, clean light switches, polish interior glass and window sills, spot clean dirt and stains from walls and remove waste.

  • The bedrooms

    Clean wardrobes, bed frames, decorative items and electronics.

  • The bathroom

    Disinfect and clean bathtub, shower and toilet bowl, clean tiles, polish mirrors, clean cabinets, descale sinks and clean taps.

  • The kitchen

    Clean cupboards, clean dirt and stains off counter tops, clean the vent, scrub hob, knobs and burners, clean refrigerator and kitchen appliances.

  • The living room

    Dust furniture (emphasis on fine details), dust all accessories, decorative items, shelves and hangings, clean electronics and vacuum upholstery.

  • Hallway & stairs

    Polish the front door and clean rugs.
