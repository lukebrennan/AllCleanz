Frequently Asked Questions Cleaning questions, answered

About All Cleanz

Where are you based and what areas do you cover?

All Cleanz is based in Prescot, Merseyside, and provides domestic and commercial cleaning throughout Merseyside and the North West, including Whiston, Rainhill, Huyton, St Helens, Kirkby, Widnes, Liverpool and Warrington. See all the areas we cover.

Do you clean homes or businesses?

Both. We offer a wide range of cleaning services for homes, offices and commercial properties, from a routine domestic clean to office cleaning contracts and builders cleans.

What cleaning services do you offer?

We offer end of tenancy cleaning, deep cleaning, domestic cleaning, carpet & upholstery cleaning, office cleaning, after build cleaning, pre & post party cleaning, oven cleaning, fridge cleaning, window cleaning, car valeting, jet washing, gutter cleaning and BBQ cleaning. See all our services.

Can I book a regular clean or just a one off?

Either. We provide a bespoke service to suit every need and budget, from regular daily, weekly or monthly cleans to a one off clean.

How do you make sure the cleaning is done properly?

To ensure the highest levels of customer satisfaction we use quality control programmes, including on-site inspections and customer surveys. For commercial contracts we also carry out frequent site supervision and quality checks.

What do your customers say about you?

We’re rated 4.9 out of 5 on Google, and 98% of people who’ve reviewed us on Facebook recommend us (139 reviews). You can read our Google reviews and Facebook reviews, or see some of them on our About page.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp us on 07721 938429, email enquiries@allcleanznorthwest.co.uk, or fill in our online quote form.

Home cleaning

What’s included in an end of tenancy clean?

We use a comprehensive end of tenancy checklist approved by agencies. The clean takes place once all your belongings have been removed and covers every room, including the kitchen, bathroom, bedrooms, living room, hallway and stairs. See the full end of tenancy checklist.

Do I need to move my belongings for a deep clean?

No. Deep cleans are done with all your belongings in place, while still reaching those neglected areas. A deep clean is ideal before selling your property, after moving in, or before a new baby arrives. See deep cleaning.

What does a regular domestic clean include?

A daily, weekly or monthly clean tailored to your needs, room by room: from dusting and wiping surfaces, vacuuming and mopping, to cleaning the bathroom, scrubbing the hob and removing rubbish. See exactly what’s included in each room.

Can you clean before or after a party?

Yes. Our pre and post party cleaning is tailormade to suit your needs and includes weekend party cleaning too. See party cleaning.

Carpets & upholstery

How long do carpets take to dry?

Using the advanced, environmentally friendly Texatherm® cleaning machine and process, carpets and upholstery dry in just 30 minutes, guaranteed.

What is Texatherm®?

Texatherm® is a patented cleaning process for carpets, upholstery and hard floors. Our Texatherm® trained technicians can switch between several cleaning methods from just one machine, including hot water extraction and low moisture cleaning, depending on what each carpet needs. See carpet and upholstery cleaning.

Can you clean office carpet tiles?

Yes. Office carpet tiles have watertight backings, so we use the Texatherm® low moisture process, which gives superior results on commercial carpet tiles, cuts cleaning time by up to 60% and causes less disruption because it’s quieter.

Ovens, fridges & BBQs

Can I use my oven straight after it’s cleaned?

Yes. Our cleaning products and techniques leave your oven spotlessly clean with no damage and minimal chemical use, and it’s safe to use straight after cleaning. We disassemble the oven and deep clean the parts. See oven cleaning.

What do you use to clean BBQs?

We use 100% non toxic, non caustic cleaning products to remove all grease, fat and carbon. We clean all types of gas and charcoal BBQs, and your BBQ is ready to use in 2 hours. See BBQ cleaning.

Why should I have my fridge cleaned?

If a fridge isn’t cleaned regularly it can become a breeding ground for bacteria that survive at low temperatures, causing smells and making food unsafe to eat. See fridge cleaning.

Outside & other services

What can you jet wash?

Driveways, patios, paving, stone walls, decking, fencing and some flat roofing systems. See jet washing, with before and after photos.

What does gutter cleaning include?

Residential and commercial gutter cleaning, unblocking drainpipes, minor gutter repairs, soffit and fascia cleaning and conservatory roof cleaning. See gutter cleaning.

Do you clean the inside of windows?

Yes. Most window cleaners don’t clean interior windows, but we provide both interior and exterior window cleaning for homes, offices, shops and other commercial spaces. See window cleaning.

Do I need to bring my car to you for valeting?

No. Our car valeting is provided as a home service, so there’s no need to wait in long queues. See car valeting.

Do you clean after building work?

Yes. We provide after build cleaning for builders and home owners, including newly built buildings, post renovation cleaning and cleaning after repairs. We’ll discuss your needs and create a cleaning schedule that suits you. See after build cleaning.
