Privacy Policy Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information All Cleanz collects when you use our website or get in touch, how we use it, and the rights you have over it. We only collect what we need to answer your enquiry, carry out our cleaning services and run our business.

Who we are

All Cleanz (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a domestic and commercial cleaning company based in Prescot, Merseyside, working across the North West of England. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us:

  • Email: enquiries@allcleanznorthwest.co.uk
  • Phone or WhatsApp: 07721 938429

The information we collect

When you ask for a quote or contact us

If you use our quote form, call us, email us or message us on WhatsApp, we collect the details you give us. This usually includes your name, email address, phone number, town or postcode, the type and size of your property, the service you’re interested in and any message you send.

When you book a clean

To carry out and invoice the work, we also keep details such as your address, how we can access the property, the services booked and any notes you give us about your home or premises.

When you apply to join the team

If you apply for a job with us, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the role you’re applying for, when you can start, and your CV along with anything else you choose to include in it.

When you visit our website

The company that hosts our website automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages visited. This is used to keep the website running securely. We do not use this information to identify you.

How we use your information

Under UK data protection law we must have a lawful reason for using your personal information. We use it:

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote. This is a step you have asked us to take before entering into a contract with us.
  • To carry out the cleaning services you book, arrange visits and send invoices. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep business and tax records. This is necessary to meet our legal obligations.
  • To consider your job application and contact you about it. This is a step you have asked us to take before entering into a contract with us.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly. This is in our legitimate interests.

We will not send you marketing messages unless you have asked us to, and we never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the people and services that help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • the company that hosts our website and handles our online forms
  • our email, phone and messaging providers, including WhatsApp if you choose to message us there
  • our accountant, and HMRC where the law requires it

Some of these providers may store information outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where that happens, they protect it with safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and quotes that don’t lead to a booking: up to 12 months.
  • Customer records and invoices: 6 years from the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for tax purposes.
  • Job applications that are not successful: up to 6 months. If you join the team, your application becomes part of your staff record.

After these periods we securely delete or destroy the information.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking. If we add website analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Our website links to other sites, such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Google. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they use your information.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to limit how we use your information, or object to us using it
  • ask for your information in a format you can take to another provider

Some of these rights only apply in certain situations. To use any of them, email or call us using the details above. We will respond within one month, and there is usually no charge.

How to complain

If you’re unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
